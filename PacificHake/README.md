## Description
This workflow is to generate the indices used for the Pacific hake Ecosystem Summary that was implmented for the 2025 assessment summary. 

### Variables produced from MOM6
$TEMP_{spawn}$: Mean temperature during spawning (shelf break, Jan-Mar, 130-500 m depth, 31-36° N) \
$AST_{egg}$: Net along-shore transport (shelf break,Jan-Mar, 40-60 m depth, 31-36° N) \
$MLD_{yolk}$: Mean mixed layer depth (shelf break Jan-Apr, 31-36° N) \
$SSH_{JA,c}$: Average sea-surface height off California as an indicator of basin-scale processes (from coast to 30 km offshore, Jan-Apr, 34.5-42.5° N) \
$MLD_{latelarv}$: Mean mixed layer depth (shelf break Mar-Jun, 31-37° N) \
$PU_{late}$: Strength of poleward undercurrent (from coast to 275 m isobath, Mar-Jun, 75-275 m depth, 33.5-34.5° N) \
